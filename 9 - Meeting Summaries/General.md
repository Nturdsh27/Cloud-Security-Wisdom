# 9 - Meeting Summaries

## Purpose

Summaries of meetings whose outcome matters beyond the people in the room — decisions, action items and context worth preserving.

## What This Folder Contains

- One page per meeting, named `YYYY-MM-DD - Subject`.
- Attendees, agenda, decisions, open questions, action items and owners.
- Links to the pages, missions or projects the meeting relates to.

## Good To Know

Write the decisions, not the transcript. If there were no decisions and no action items, the meeting probably does not need a page.

## Structure

This folder follows the standard layout defined in `10 - Extras/Templates/Content Folders Structure - Template`. Copy the skeleton from there — do not invent a layout.

```text
<Folder>/
+-- General.md
+-- YYYY/
    +-- General.md
    +-- YYYY-MM-DD - Subject.md
    +-- Recurring Series Name/
        +-- General.md
        +-- YYYY-MM-DD - Subject.md
```

- One page per meeting, named `YYYY-MM-DD - Subject`. The date sorts the folder; the subject tells people what it was.
- A meeting that repeats gets a series folder; a one-off sits directly in the year folder.
