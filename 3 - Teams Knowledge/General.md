# 3 - Teams Knowledge

## Purpose

Knowledge owned by a specific team. Each team maintains its own folder, following the same hierarchy so that moving between teams does not mean learning a new filing system.

## What This Folder Contains

- One folder per team: `AI Security & Wiz`, `Application Security`, `Cloud Security AWS`, `Cloud Security GCP & Azure`, `DefenSys`, `Light Rail`, `Ocean 19`.
- Every team folder follows the standard skeleton: `Hafifa`, `Missions`, `Projects`.
- The reference skeleton lives in `10 - Extras/Templates/Team Organization of Knowledge - Tempalte`.

## Good To Know

Team folders are **owned** by their team, but **readable by everyone** — that is how we avoid silos. Changes to a team folder should be reviewed by someone from that team.

Note the difference in scope: `Missions`, `Studies`, `Presentations`, `Architectures` and `Meeting Summaries` also exist at department level (folders `4`-`9`). Same conventions, different audience: team level is team-internal work, department level is work that concerns the whole department.
