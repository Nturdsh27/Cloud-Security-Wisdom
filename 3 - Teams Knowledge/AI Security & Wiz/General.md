# AI Security & Wiz

## Purpose

The knowledge base of the **AI Security & Wiz** team: who we are, what we are responsible for, and everything we have learned while doing it.

## What This Folder Contains

- `Hafifa` — the team's onboarding track: what a new member must learn, in what order.
- `How To` — step-by-step guides for recurring tasks: how to actually do the things this team does.
- `Missions` — team-level mission records.
- `Projects` — the team's ongoing and completed projects, each with its own full structure.
- Team-specific know-how: environments, access, routines, contacts and responsibilities.

## Good To Know

This folder follows the standard team skeleton defined in `10 - Extras/Templates/Team Organization of Knowledge - Tempalte`. Keep the structure identical — predictability is the point.

Scope reminder: items here are **team level**. Work that concerns the whole department belongs in folders `4`-`9` at the root.
