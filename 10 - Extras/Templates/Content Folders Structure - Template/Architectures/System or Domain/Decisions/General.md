# Architecture Decisions

## Purpose

Why this system is built the way it is. One page per significant decision, written when the decision is made.

## What This Folder Contains

- One decision record per page: context, options considered, decision, consequences, status.
- Superseded decisions stay here, marked as superseded and linked to the one that replaced them.

## Naming

`NNNN - <Decision>.md`, numbered sequentially: `0003 - Centralize Logs In One Account.md`.

## Good To Know

- Record the **options you rejected and why**. That is the part people need two years later, when someone proposes the rejected option again.
- Never edit a decision to change its outcome. Write a new one that supersedes it — the history is the point.
