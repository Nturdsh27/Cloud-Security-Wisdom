# Meeting Summaries

## Purpose

Meetings whose outcome matters beyond the room: decisions, action items and the context behind them.

## What This Folder Contains

- One folder per year, and inside it one page per meeting.
- Recurring meeting series get their own folder inside the year.

## Structure

```text
Meeting Summaries/
+-- General.md
+-- YYYY/
    +-- General.md
    +-- YYYY-MM-DD - Subject.md
    +-- Recurring Series Name/
        +-- General.md
        +-- YYYY-MM-DD - Subject.md
```

## Naming

- Year folder: `2026`.
- Meeting page: `YYYY-MM-DD - Subject.md`. The date sorts the folder for you; the subject tells people what it was.

## Good To Know

- **Write decisions, not transcripts.** If there were no decisions and no action items, the meeting probably does not need a page.
- **Every action item needs an owner and a date.** An action item without an owner is a wish.
- Link the meeting to the mission, project or report it affected — in both directions.
- Meetings are where sensitive things get said out loud. Summarize what was decided, not everything that was mentioned.
