# Study Experiments

## Purpose

What was actually tried, under what conditions, and what happened.

## What This Folder Contains

- One page per experiment: hypothesis, setup, steps, result, interpretation.
- Scripts and configurations used, with placeholders instead of real identifiers.
- Failed and inconclusive attempts $em they belong here too.

## Naming

`<NN> - <What Was Tried>.md`, numbered in the order they were run.

## Good To Know

Record the environment and the date: a result is only valid for the version of the service you tested.
