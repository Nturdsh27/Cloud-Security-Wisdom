# 0 - General

## Purpose

Department-level, non-technical information. This is the first stop for anyone joining the department — who we are, how we work, and the language we speak.

## What This Folder Contains

- Department structure, teams and areas of responsibility.
- Ways of working: routines, rituals, reporting lines, escalation paths.
- Onboarding checklists and first-week guides.
- Internal terminology and acronyms used across the department.
- Recurring processes and administrative how-tos.

## Good To Know

If a piece of knowledge is technical, it does not belong here — it belongs in `1 - Basic Knowledge For Cloud`, `2 - Tools & Platforms`, or the relevant team folder.
