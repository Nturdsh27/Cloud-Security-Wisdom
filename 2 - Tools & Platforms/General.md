# 2 - Tools & Platforms

## Purpose

The operational manual for every tool and platform we actually use. Not marketing material — practical, reproducible instructions.

## What This Folder Contains

- One page (or sub-folder) per tool: what it is and why we use it.
- How to get access, authenticate and configure it.
- The common tasks, with real commands and queries.
- Known limitations, gotchas and workarounds.
- Useful saved queries, dashboards and integrations.

## Good To Know

Date anything version-dependent ("As of 11-11-2026 the console does X"). Tools change faster than our documentation does.
